### Name (EN)
StartPOZI Privacy Guard

### Summary (EN, до 250 знаков)
Ad & tracker blocker, automatic cookie-banner rejection, URL tracking cleanup,
DNT + Global Privacy Control and anti-fingerprinting — the full privacy stack of
StartPOZI Browser in one extension.

### Description (EN)
The privacy protection of StartPOZI Browser, brought to Firefox.

★ AD & TRACKER BLOCKER
14 curated FilterHub lists (EasyList, EasyPrivacy, the uBlock core, Peter Lowe,
NoCoin, OISD, URLhaus…) with Light / Medium / Maximum presets and custom filters
in EasyList format. A built-in offline base of 113,000 rules starts blocking from
the very first second — before any list is even downloaded.

★ AUTOMATIC COOKIE-BANNER REJECTION
~70 CMP providers, shadow DOM support, multilingual heuristics — banners are
dismissed without ever clicking "Accept".

★ URL TRACKING CLEANUP
Strips utm_*, fbclid, gclid, msclkid and dozens of other tracking parameters at
the network level and in the visible address.

★ DNT + GLOBAL PRIVACY CONTROL
Sends DNT and Sec-GPC: 1 headers on every request and exposes
navigator.globalPrivacyControl — the complete GPC opt-out.

★ ANTI-FINGERPRINTING
Canvas noise, WebGL masking, audio farbling, WebRTC IP-leak blocking, media
device and font masking — ported from StartPOZI Browser.

★ MORE
Third-party cookie stripping, referrer trimmed to origin, <a ping>/beacon
blocking, an element zapper ("Block ads manually"), per-site protection toggle,
a shield popup with live per-site stats, and settings styled after the browser.
StartPOZI becomes your default search engine.

Privacy: no telemetry, no analytics, no data sent anywhere. Everything stays in
your browser. External requests are limited to filter-list updates (GitHub),
the daily Bing picture of the day, and startpozi.com search.

## ── Вопросник о данных (Data Collection Disclosure) ────────────
На все вопросы «собирает ли расширение…» — ответ НЕТ (не собирает):
- Does it collect technical data? → No
- Does it collect interaction data? → No
- Does it collect personally identifying data? → No
- Does it use third-party services? → Yes (только загрузка публичных данных):
  • raw.githubusercontent.com — обновление списков фильтров
  • www.bing.com — картинка дня для фона настроек
  • startpozi.com — поисковая система (только при поиске)
  Ни на один из них НЕ отправляются данные пользователя.

## ── Reviewer notes (заметки ревьюеру, EN) ──────────────────────
This add-on is a port of the privacy stack from our open-source browser
(github.com/StartPOZI/StartPOZI-Desktop-Browser, GPL-3.0). Permissions:
webRequest/webRequestBlocking — request blocking and header injection
(DNT, Sec-GPC, Referrer) exactly as in the browser; cookies — cookie counter
in the popup; browsingData — the optional "clear cookies/cache on exit"
setting; tabs — per-tab shield stats and badge. No remote code is executed;
the CSP on extension pages forbids it. Source: this repository.
